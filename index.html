How it works

Three steps. No migration. No agents run wild.

Thellador never touches your code. It reads the record of what your team already wrote down — and connects the dots no human can hold in their head.

01

Connect the repo

Read-only token, two clicks. Thellador pulls commit messages, PR descriptions, review threads, linked issues and incident notes — never the source itself.

02

It builds the story graph

Causality, not keywords: which commit answered which outage, which revert undid which fix, which “temporary” hack has outlived three authors.

03

Ask anything

From your terminal, a PR comment, or Slack. Every answer arrives with commits, PRs and people cited — click through and verify it yourself.

What you get

Blame turned into testimony.

⌖

Answers with citations

Not “it looks like…” — every claim links the exact commit, PR and incident it came from. If the history doesn’t know, Thellador says so.

⟳

Onboarding in one afternoon

New hire asks “what does this module actually do, and who owns it?” — gets the human history: why it exists, what it replaced, which fires it survived.

⚔

Incident archaeology

During an outage: “what changed in this path this week?” Thellador reconstructs the change timeline across PRs and reverts before the postmortem starts.

⚿

Your history stays yours

Read-only access, ephemeral index, per-repo scoping. Nothing is written back, nothing is shared, nothing trains anything. Ask, answer, forget.
